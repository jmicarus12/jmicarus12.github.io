High River Mobile Tire Services

hrmobiletires.ca · mobile tire service, High River, Alberta

Live

A complete booking platform for a mobile tire business in Alberta: public marketing site, customer accounts, and an admin back office. Because the van drives to the customer, the booking flow has to capture exactly where that is. Built on the current Laravel stack — Laravel 12, Livewire 4 and Flux 2 — and deployed to Hostinger.

  • Guest booking with no account required: contact details, date and time, service, tire brand and size
  • Service address captured field-by-field and pinned to latitude/longitude on a Leaflet map
  • Customer accounts through Google sign-in, so there are no passwords to store, plus Fortify two-factor auth, recovery codes and email verification
  • Booking history and detail views for signed-in customers
  • Separate admin guard and login, with its own dashboard
  • Appointment workflow — accept, cancel, complete — managed from the back office
  • Admin CRUD for tire services (with deactivate/reactivate), customer stories, review stats and booking settings
  • Photo album with drag-to-reorder image management
  • New-booking alerts pushed straight to Telegram, avoiding a paid SMS gateway
  • SEO meta, canonical and Open Graph tags, plus light and dark appearance

HANDS

Telecom site infrastructure & maintenance management

Maintenance & features

A large operations system for telecom sites, maintained for Digitus (formerly Hayat Communications). 67 models covering the physical plant at each site and the work orders that keep it running. My brief was ongoing maintenance, new features and performance work — done off-hours so field operations never paused.

  • Site inventory: locations, floors, cabinets, sharing and domains
  • Power and cooling plant — DC systems, batteries, UPS, mains, standby gensets, free-cooling units, air conditioning, fire systems
  • Trouble tickets with priority, status and resolution codes
  • Snag tracking by category, severity and owner
  • Preventive-maintenance planner with progress tracking
  • Leave planner, balances and review workflow for staff
  • Real-time notifications over WebSockets, dashboards in Chart.js
  • GeoJSON backbone mapping and Geotab fleet telematics

DNA

Product distribution / MLM platform

Core functionality

A multi-level marketing and product distribution platform — the downline and stock side of the business, not a storefront. Started on Laravel 6 with a service-layer architecture, later rebuilt on Laravel 8 with Livewire on a Paper Dashboard base.

  • Product catalogue with change history
  • Distribution stock and distribution-history ledgers
  • Service-layer architecture: BaseService, ProductService, DistributionService, UserService
  • Roles and permissions per distributor level
  • Server-side DataTables for large listings
  • Error monitoring wired to Bugsnag

Self-testing static-hosting demo

Side project

A small teaching site that probes its own host at runtime to show what static hosting can and cannot do — live HTTPS check, a JSON file used as a read-only API, a rejected POST, and a PHP file served as plain text to prove nothing executes server-side.

  • Eight live capability probes, run in the visitor's browser
  • Published quotas and a can/can't reference table
  • Jekyll vs. .nojekyll, base paths and custom domains explained
  • No framework, no build step, no dependencies
