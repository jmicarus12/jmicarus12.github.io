This is a demonstration, not a payroll system. It runs entirely in your browser — there is no server, no database and no client data here. Everything you type is saved to this browser's localStorage and to nowhere else. The figures are illustrative; do not pay anyone from them.

How it is calculated

Three of these are genuinely counter-intuitive, and all three came out of how the agency actually pays people.

Four hours earns a full day

A shift of 4 hours or more credits one whole day at the daily rate. A guard who renders 5 hours is paid the same base as one who renders 8. Under 4 hours credits nothing, though the hours still count toward the total rendered.

Overtime is its own rate

Anything past the 8th hour is paid at a flat overtime rate per hour set in the contract — not a multiplier of the daily rate.

Contributions are monthly, not per cut-off

Cash bond comes off every cut-off. SSS, Pag-IBIG and PhilHealth only come off the cut-off that ends the month, so a 1–15 payslip looks lighter than a 16–31 one.

Night differential

A shift falling inside the night window earns the flat night rate; one that only overlaps it earns a pro-rated share, at the night rate divided by eight per hour.
